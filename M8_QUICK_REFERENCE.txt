## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| **Section 2 too slow** | Video_Data on slow drive; copy to local SSD |
| **Memory error** | Close apps; reduce batch_size to 4/2 |
| **Training crashes** | Embeddings corrupted; delete & re-extract |
| **Can't find embeddings** | Check `models/m8_embeddings/` folder exists |
| **GPU errors** | Ensure CUDA disabled (should be using CPU) |
